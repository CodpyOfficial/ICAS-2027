Tutorials support lifelong learning and professional development. They may cover emerging research directions or established techniques with practical or industrial relevance within the scope of autonomous systems.

{{icon "info"}}

Tutorial or paper with workshop? A paper with workshop is a refereed contribution with a 25-minute presentation and a 90-minute workshop, whose speakers receive 40% of the workshop registration fees as feedback. A tutorial is an educational session proposed through this call.

Submission guidelines

The proposal must give the tutorial chairs enough information to judge the relevance, quality and expected impact of the tutorial. Formats and durations will be confirmed when the call opens.

Required information

  • Tutorial title and abstract.
  • Motivation and expected impact — who should attend and what they will take away.
  • Tutorial outline — the syllabus and the time allocated to each part.
  • Speakers — names, affiliations, and which part each one presents.
  • Short biography of the main speaker (and optionally the others).

Evaluation criteria

  • {{icon "award"}} Technical quality

    Quality of the content and relevance to {{.Site.Conference.Acronym}}.

  • {{icon "clock"}} Timeliness & impact

    Importance of the topic and its potential impact.

  • {{icon "graduation"}} Educational value

    Benefit to attendees with different backgrounds.

  • {{icon "user"}} Speaker expertise

    Experience of the speakers in the topic and in teaching it.

  • {{icon "list"}} Clarity of structure

    Completeness and clarity of the outline.
