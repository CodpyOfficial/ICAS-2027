{{.Site.Conference.FullTitle}} invites proposals for special sessions that complement the regular program with emerging topics and innovative applications of autonomous systems.

Proposals should address timely, high-impact themes. Interdisciplinary and multidisciplinary topics are especially encouraged, as are sessions that tackle open problems or novel application domains outside the main conference theme. Papers in special sessions are peer reviewed with the same standards as regular submissions.

Example topics

For example:

  • Foundation models, LLMs and embodied AI for autonomous agents
  • Certification, assurance and regulation of learning-enabled systems
  • Autonomy in space, maritime, agriculture and other extreme or remote environments
  • Human–robot and human–vehicle interaction, trust and explainability
  • Benchmarks, simulators and digital twins for reproducible autonomy research
  • Other themes within the conference scope are also welcome

Proposal contents

  1. Title and abstract — the topic and scope of the session.
  2. Rationale — why the topic is novel and timely, and why it matters to the {{.Site.Conference.Acronym}} community.
  3. Proposed speakers and talks — the list of talks with speakers and co-authors. Invited industry speakers are viewed positively.
  4. Short biographies of the session organizers.

Page limits and the expected number of papers per session will be confirmed when the call opens.

Evaluation criteria

  • {{icon "compass"}} Relevance

    Fit with the scope of autonomous systems and with the conference audience.

  • {{icon "clock"}} Timeliness

    Importance of the topic now, and its potential impact.

  • {{icon "award"}} Quality & track record

    Expertise of the organizers and proposed speakers.

  • {{icon "layers"}} Coherence & added value

    How well the talks form a session and complement the regular program.
