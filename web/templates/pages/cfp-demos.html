Live demonstrations let attendees see, try, interact with and challenge working autonomous systems: software running on a laptop, a robot or vehicle, or a system interacting with people.

Demonstrations run in booths during the conference. An award for the best live demonstration is planned; details will be announced with the call.

Demo description

Each submission is a short description of the demonstration. It should be based on published work being demonstrated for the first time, or on a paper submitted to {{.Site.Conference.FullTitle}}.

Required content

  1. Title and reference. Start the title with “Live Demonstration:” and give at least one reference (a URL is recommended) to the published paper, or the submission ID of your {{.Site.Conference.Acronym}} proposal.
  2. Demonstration set-up. The equipment used, a photo or screenshot, and any special requirements (space, power, network, safety perimeter for moving robots or drones). Demonstrators bring their own equipment.
  3. Visitor experience. How visitors will interact with the demo, what they will learn, and how it shows the essence of the work.
  4. Video (optional). A link to a video of at most 5 minutes. It supports the review and does not replace the live demonstration.
