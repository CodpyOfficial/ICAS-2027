About

About {{$c.Acronym}}

The {{$c.Name}} ({{$c.Acronym}}) is a forum for researchers, engineers and practitioners working on the theory, design and deployment of systems that perceive, decide and act on their own — from robots and vehicles to self-adaptive software.

{{$c.FullTitle}} combines two complementary formats: papers with workshop, where speakers present in the morning and run a 90-minute interactive workshop in the afternoon, and papers without workshop, presented as focused 15-minute talks.

  • {{icon "check"}}Presentations and laptop-based workshops (papers with workshop)
  • {{icon "check"}}Paper sessions (papers without workshop)
  • {{icon "check"}}Keynote talks, special sessions and tutorials
  • {{icon "check"}}Live demonstrations and industry exhibition

The conference theme puts implementation first — autonomous robotics, vehicles, embedded computing, perception, simulation and real-world applications — and extends to control, planning, learning, multi-agent systems, human–autonomy teaming, safety, security and the ethics and policy questions autonomy raises.

All proposals undergo double-blind peer review for technical depth and originality, clarity and feasibility, engagement, and alignment with current industry and academic trends.
